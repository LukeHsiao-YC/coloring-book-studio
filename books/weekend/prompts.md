# What Shall We Do This Weekend?：每頁提示詞

在 Gemini 或 ChatGPT 開一個新對話，第一則訊息先上傳角色參考圖：reference.jpg、lineart_sheet.jpg，然後依序貼上每一頁。

## p01：What shall we do this weekend?

```
Children's coloring book page. Pure black line art on a pure white background. No shading, no gray tones, no color, no gradients, no hatching, no texture fill. Every area must be a closed shape that a child can color in. Portrait orientation, 3:4 aspect ratio. No border or frame. Leave a clear white margin on all four sides: nothing may touch or be cut off by the edges of the image. The image MUST be a tall portrait (taller than wide, 3:4), never landscape. Compose for a tall page: the characters are large and fill the middle, with more sky or wall above them and more ground or floor below them.

Coloring page for ages 6 to 9. Medium-weight clean black outlines. Characters plus a simple scene background with at most 6 background objects. Keep every coloring area at least fingertip-sized. No scattered small decorations: no floating leaves, no sprinkled grass tufts, no confetti, no dense patterns.

Characters (match the attached reference images exactly; the line-art sheet shows the target style):
- Xiao Hei (the dark gray cat): very tall pointed ears, a little fluffy fur on the cheeks, large oval eyes with flat half-closed upper lids (a cool, unimpressed look), tiny "w"-shaped mouth.
- Xiao Bai (the white cat): very tall pointed ears, a little fluffy fur on the cheeks, huge round open eyes, three short blush lines on each cheek, tiny "w"-shaped mouth.
- Xiao Huang (the tabby cat): shorter rounder ears, softly rounded head, gently closed eyes drawn as short lines, a few short stripes on the forehead, cheeks and legs, tiny "w"-shaped mouth, relaxed happy face.
Style: simple, cute, hand-drawn look with thick even black outlines, exactly like the attached line-art sheet. Body shape: chibi proportions with a big head about as large as the body, a boxy body, short stubby legs, and round mitten-like paws with no fingers drawn. Keep the bodies simple and boxy, not realistic cats. All three wear the same plain white boxy zip-up jacket (one vertical zipper line with a small arrow-shaped zipper pull at the top) and plain shorts. The jacket has a small chest logo on the right side of the zipper that must be copied EXACTLY as it appears in the reference images (one Korean character followed by the letters CAT); never mirror, misspell or omit it unless it is hidden by the pose. Tails are not shown in the reference; if a pose shows a tail, keep it short and simple.

Scene: Living room. Xiao Hei lies on the sofa looking bored at a smartphone, Xiao Bai sits on the floor watching a TV with a blank screen, looking sleepy. Xiao Huang stands at a big window, pointing outside with an excited face. Background: sofa, TV on a low cabinet, window with a tree outside, one rug.

No text on the page other than the chest logo on the jackets.
```
