# What Shall We Do This Weekend?：每頁提示詞

在 Gemini 或 ChatGPT 開一個新對話，第一則訊息先上傳角色參考圖：reference.png、lineart_sheet.jpg，然後依序貼上每一頁。

## p01：What shall we do this weekend?

```
Children's coloring book page. Pure black line art on a pure white background. No shading, no gray tones, no color, no gradients, no hatching, no texture fill. Every area must be a closed shape that a child can color in. Portrait orientation, 3:4 aspect ratio. No border or frame. Leave a clear white margin on all four sides: nothing may touch or be cut off by the edges of the image.

Coloring page for ages 6 to 9. Medium-weight clean black outlines. Characters plus a simple scene background with at most 6 background objects. Keep every coloring area at least fingertip-sized. No scattered small decorations: no floating leaves, no sprinkled grass tufts, no confetti, no dense patterns.

Characters (match the attached reference images exactly; the line-art sheet shows the target style):
- Xiao Hei (the dark gray cat): fluffy cheek tufts, pointed ears, half-closed confident eyes, small smirk, long tail. Cool, playful personality.
- Xiao Bai (the white cat): big round eyes, small blush marks on cheeks, sweet shy expression, thin curled tail.
- Xiao Huang (the tabby cat): stripes on forehead, cheeks and legs, happy closed eyes, relaxed smile, striped tail.
All cats wear the same white zip-up jacket and shorts. The jacket has a small chest logo that must be copied EXACTLY as it appears in the reference images (one Korean character followed by the letters CAT); never mirror, misspell or omit it unless it is hidden by the pose. Same proportions as the reference: big head, small body, short legs.

Scene: Living room. Xiao Hei lies on the sofa looking bored at a smartphone, Xiao Bai sits on the floor watching a TV with a blank screen, looking sleepy. Xiao Huang stands at a big window, pointing outside with an excited face. Background: sofa, TV on a low cabinet, window with a tree outside, one rug.

No text on the page other than the chest logo on the jackets.
```
